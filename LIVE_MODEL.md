## Narrative spine
Working title: The Geometry of Existence. Seven books, chromatic book ordinal Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Violet, White. Philosophical high fantasy: discovery versus certainty, preservation versus change; no binary good-versus-evil or obligatory chosen-one Black Prophecy. Aren, female human cartographer, approximately 25, finds the same mountain in two geographically incompatible locations. The Conservator, male human master Architect, approximately 60, maintains an inherited velis network preserving a geometric configuration of the White material realm. His interventions have protected civilization but may suppress an admissible gradual transition. Aren investigates six realms, learns different weaving traditions, and eventually seeks a safe coordinated transition. Abrupt dismantling would genuinely be catastrophic; cooperation is necessary. Final scene: Aren returns to the mountain and tells an apprentice her new map 'will be correct for the world as it is today.' The network's exact physics, origin, and transition conditions remain OPEN, not AERA theorems.

## Chromatic structure and populations
AERA six basins Red/Orange/Yellow/Green/Blue/Violet; White superposition, not seventh chromatic basin. This implementation chooses six inhabited chromatic realms (each 80% dominant basin, 20% others) and White material realm with ordinary local spatial experience. Chromatic realms can have globally nonordinary geometry, locally coherent ground/sky and orientation. Native realm access: each chromatic race may enter its own realm and White, not other chromatic realms. White humans can enter all six. Native races can express humanoid biology in White while preserving chromatic identity, native form and access constraints; Vaelith living Genesis system serves as possible biological blueprint. Six races: Red Dragons; Orange **Vaelith** (V-A-E-L-I-T-H); Yellow Primordials; Green Sprites; Blue Leviathans; Violet Unicorns. White includes human population expressions, potentially dwarf-like and elf-like. Dragon proposed sizes: juveniles 8–20 ft, adults 40–80 ft, ancients 100–150 ft; working story values, not framework law.

## Topological Weaving
Weaving is a Transduction specialization, not a replacement magic system. Proposed relational techniques: connection, separation, redirection, boundary, folding and closure, always subject to AERA admissibility. Field-native anchors are **velis** (V-E-L-I-S), direct localized Genesis-field topologies formed by Architects, not physical sigils. `ru’ne` are individual physical geometric inscriptions; complete sigils are physical arrangements on a substrate; sigil arrays coordinate them. Weavers may operate directly at available Genesis-field resolution/bandwidth without any physical object, or employ physical sigils and prepared cards. Velis persistence depends on coherence/load and maintenance. AERA's Conceive → Shape → Hold → Resolve → Manifest → Release lifecycle and basin/fatigue/bandwidth constraints apply. Optional disciplinary traditions: Dragon relational, Vaelith symbiotic, Primordial morphological, Sprite potential, Leviathan persistent, Unicorn adjacency, human integrative. These are cultural specializations, NOT exclusive racial powers. Proposed symbolic anchor A=(P,F,C,gamma), weave W=(X,R,boundary X) are explanatory placeholders, not derived L0 formulas.
