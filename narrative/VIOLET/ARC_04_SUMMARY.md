# VIOLET — Arc 04: The Original Weave

**Book:** The Cartographers  
**Pass:** 1 — accepted narrative skeleton  
**Setting:** Violet Realm research archives; Elarith Directorate archives through authorized access  
**Characters:** Aren, Aelira, Conservator, Seren, Unicorn and Directorate researchers  
**Purpose:** Reconstruct foundational design principles without treating the original configuration as timeless perfection.

## Five major plot points

### 1. Oldest surviving maps

Fragmentary independent charts and maintenance records are compared; no complete original blueprint conveniently survives.

### 2. Foundational architecture

Researchers infer an original organization with relatively modular relationships and greater independent local adjustability, complicated by later cross-dependencies.

### 3. Not a perfect past

The Conservator stresses the original design suited historical environmental conditions; later adaptations were often necessary and reasonable.

### 4. Candidate alternatives

Combining global geometry, transformation analysis, and configuration space identifies promising configurations that reduce some dependencies, but no verified safe transition yet exists.

### 5. Conservator’s recognition

The Conservator acknowledges the findings while requiring continuity of essential services, demonstrated safe transitions, and legitimate multi-government authorization.

## Transition

Convene the six independent chromatic disciplines to synthesize the network analysis.
