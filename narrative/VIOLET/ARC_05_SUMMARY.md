# VIOLET — Arc 05: The Sixfold Revelation

**Book:** The Cartographers  
**Pass:** 1 — accepted narrative skeleton  
**Setting:** Stormwall Kingdom, White Realm; Violet specialized research through Aren and Aelira  
**Characters:** Aren, Aelira, Tharos, Saelith, Orun, Iri, Vaelor, Conservator, Seren  
**Purpose:** Unify six research traditions without inventing a new energy basin, prophecy, or universally safe solution.

## Five major plot points

### 1. Gathering in White

Because chromatic natives cannot freely enter other chromatic realms, all six meet in Stormwall in White; Aren and Aelira bring Violet research there.

### 2. Six complementary disciplines

Tharos: spatial continuity; Saelith: evolving boundaries; Orun: transformation and preservation; Iri: reachability; Vaelor: long-duration provenance; Aelira: global chart compatibility. Aren integrates their evidence.

### 3. Inherited incompatibility

Analysis indicates environmental evolution plus locally justified compensating relationships has narrowed global adaptability and generated growing interdependent constraints; not all regions are proven identical.

### 4. Different architecture possible

Candidate reorganizations might restore some subsystem independence while preserving current functions, but their transition paths and global consequences remain unverified.

### 5. Return to White

The Conservator supports formal authorized research, not immediate implementation; Seren identifies cross-sovereign authority as unresolved. An Elarith international assembly is proposed for White.

## Transition

White — Arc 01: The Return to White, Elarith international assembly and authorized network reconfiguration inquiry.

## Continuity / boundaries

- This is an accepted arc-level progression, **not** chapter architecture or final manuscript.
- The atlas is an evolving mathematical/cartographic representation, not six new magical powers.
- Network deterioration is due to environmental evolution interacting with accumulated engineering constraints. It is distinct from Starscape-driven Genesis storms, Domai, and Eldris and from independent municipal stabilization arrays.
