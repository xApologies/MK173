# VIOLET — Arc 02: The Unicorn Cartographers

**Book:** The Cartographers  
**Pass:** 1 — accepted narrative skeleton  
**Setting:** Violet Realm; Unicorn city and cartographers’ guild  
**Characters:** Aren, Aelira, Unicorn cartographers  
**Purpose:** Immerse Aren in mature Unicorn cartography and develop a reciprocal collaboration.

## Five major plot points

### 1. The unmappable city

The city’s districts occupy locally coherent but globally non-ordinary connected regions; ordinary planar projections misstate navigable adjacency.

### 2. Cartographers’ guild

The Unicorn institution maintains overlapping local charts, surveying standards, historical changes, and explicit transition relationships.

### 3. The contradictory survey

Aren surveys a closed multi-district route; each local measurement is valid, but her attempted single global coordinate reconstruction appears contradictory.

### 4. Overlapping-chart atlas

Aelira teaches that a global atlas can be an organized family of local charts with explicit transition relations, not one undistorted picture.

### 5. Professional disagreement

Unicorn researchers disagree about whether richer global reconstruction is worthwhile; Aren and Aelira receive approval for a joint project preserving local accuracy.

## Transition

Investigate global relationships revealed by composing transitions around complete routes.
