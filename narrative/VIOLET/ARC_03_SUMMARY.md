# VIOLET — Arc 03: The Geometry Beyond Distance

**Book:** The Cartographers  
**Pass:** 1 — accepted narrative skeleton  
**Setting:** Violet Realm; connected research landscape  
**Characters:** Aren, Aelira, Unicorn research team, Directorate correspondents  
**Purpose:** Produce Violet’s principal mathematical breakthrough while retaining evidential limits.

## Five major plot points

### 1. Atlas without a center

Aren and Aelira reject a privileged reference region and represent global structure through locally valid charts and their transition relationships.

### 2. Closed-route orientation

A controlled closed route returns to its starting location with a nontrivial transported reference-frame orientation, revealing information not captured by distance alone.

### 3. Composed transitions

Different closed routes show distinct accumulated effects; local validity alone does not determine global compatibility.

### 4. Correction of dimension inference

Aren initially attributes nontrivial orientation to an extra spatial dimension; Aelira demonstrates that this observation alone does not establish extra physical dimensions.

### 5. Global relational atlas

The combined framework represents local charts, transitions, closed-route behavior, and selected global compatibility conditions; network records suggest previously obscured relationships.

## Transition

Apply the global atlas to reconstruct the ancient network’s foundational geometry.
