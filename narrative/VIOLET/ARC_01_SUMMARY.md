# VIOLET — Arc 01: The Inverted Realm

**Book:** The Cartographers  
**Pass:** 1 — accepted narrative skeleton  
**Setting:** Azure League → Stormwall Kingdom (Tyranor) → Violet Realm  
**Characters:** Aren, Aelira, Crown Princess Seliane Veylorn, Stormwall navigation authority  
**Purpose:** Introduce Stormwall, Aelira, and the insufficiency of a single global coordinate representation.

## Five major plot points

### 1. Journey to Stormwall

Following Blue’s network assessment, Aren travels under Directorate and royal navigation authority sponsorship to Stormwall; her research access is authorized and bounded.

### 2. Royal cartographic archive

Stormwall archives document locally accurate yet globally non-ordinary geometry. Seliane facilitates institutional access without joining Aren as a permanent companion.

### 3. Meeting Aelira

Unicorn cartographer Aelira identifies Aren’s unwarranted assumption that locally valid relations can always be combined into one ordinary global coordinate frame.

### 4. Inversion experiment

A controlled demonstration shows a transported orientation can change across local frames; an ordinary rotation in one frame does not fully describe the global relationship.

### 5. Crossing into Violet

Aren and Aelira enter Violet and encounter locally navigable but globally inverted connections; Aren must retain chart-transition data to make a faithful atlas.

## Transition

Aelira introduces Aren to the Unicorn cartographic institution.
