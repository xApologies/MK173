# VIOLET — The Cartographers | Pass One Live Model v0.1

**Status: ACCEPTED (five arc-level plot skeletons).**

## Arc registry

- **01 — The Inverted Realm:** Introduce Stormwall, Aelira, and the insufficiency of a single global coordinate representation.
- **02 — The Unicorn Cartographers:** Immerse Aren in mature Unicorn cartography and develop a reciprocal collaboration.
- **03 — The Geometry Beyond Distance:** Produce Violet’s principal mathematical breakthrough while retaining evidential limits.
- **04 — The Original Weave:** Reconstruct foundational design principles without treating the original configuration as timeless perfection.
- **05 — The Sixfold Revelation:** Unify six research traditions without inventing a new energy basin, prophecy, or universally safe solution.

## Book-wide causal trajectory

1. Stormwall and Aelira reveal that a single global coordinate chart is insufficient.
2. Unicorn cartography supplies overlapping local charts and explicit transition relationships.
3. Closed-route behavior and composed transitions reveal global properties obscured by isolated local surveys.
4. Fragmentary ancient records suggest a more modular original network; later necessary repairs increased coupling.
5. Six research traditions are synthesized in White; inherited incompatibilities are identified, but safe reconfiguration and political authorization remain unresolved.

## Recurring character development

- **Aren:** Human cartographer and developing Weaver; becomes integrator of six specialized disciplines, not a universal expert or prophet.
- **Aelira:** Independent Unicorn cartographer and Aren's closest professional counterpart; rigorous about local charts and global compatibility.
- **Tharos:** Dragon Architect, spatial continuity.
- **Saelith:** Vaelith researcher, living-system continuity and adaptation.
- **Orun:** Primordial Architect, transformations and preserved properties.
- **Iri:** Sprite researcher, recursive configurations and reachability.
- **Vaelor:** Leviathan scholar, persistence, provenance, inherited dependencies.
- **Conservator:** Moves from preserving current operation toward conditional support for an authorized, evidence-based reconfiguration investigation.
- **Seren:** Directorate liaison; foregrounds intergovernmental consent and accountability.
- **Seliane Veylorn:** Stormwall crown princess and royal cartographic sponsor; not an automatic party member.

## Realm-access constraint — essential

The six chromatic natives **do not gather in Violet**. Each can visit their native realm and White, but cannot freely travel into other chromatic realms. The sixfold meeting is in **Stormwall, White Realm**; Aren and Aelira conduct Violet research and bring findings to the gathering.

## White transition

The findings motivate a proposed international assembly in **Elarith Republic**. No globally safe transition is proven; the Conservator has not authorized implementation. White opens with political and engineering evaluation, not a preordained victory.

## AERA continuity

- Chromatic book colors organize literary sequence, not character power acquisition.
- The ancient network is engineered; its deterioration is environmental evolution interacting with accumulated engineering constraints.
- Municipal Genesis-field stabilization is independent of the ancient network.
- Genesis storms, Domai, Eldris arise from Starscape singularity / local Genesis-field conditions, not network deterioration.
- The Violet realm's non-ordinary geometry is native, not caused by the network.
- Local-chart topology and closed-loop reference-frame behavior do **not** alone establish extra physical dimensions.
