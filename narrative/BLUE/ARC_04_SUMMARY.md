# BLUE — Arc 04: The Conservator’s Truth
**Project:** MK173 | **Book:** Blue — *What Endures* | **Pass:** 1 (accepted plot skeleton)
**Setting:** Azure League; Elarith Directorate records
**Arc purpose:** Reframe the Conservator as competent, accountable, cautious, and limited; shift opposition toward conditional cooperation.

## Five accepted major plot points
1. **The Conservator travels to Azure with Seren and senior engineers after reviewing Aren and Vaelor's work; he asks how to change the network without interrupting vital services.**
2. **He opens restricted Directorate history showing previous partial interventions, local successes, and dangerous transitions.**
3. **He recounts an intervention he supported that encountered an unrecognized intermediate dependency, disrupting transport for an extended period before stabilization.**
4. **Aren recognizes the configuration-space failure; the Conservator recognizes that her atlas could improve evaluation but does not guarantee safety.**
5. **The Directorate acknowledges the network's deteriorating maintenance outlook and authorizes conditional joint noninvasive research; Aren and the Conservator remain divided on acceptable intervention risk.**
