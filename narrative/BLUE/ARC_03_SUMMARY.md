# BLUE — Arc 03: The Cost of Persistence
**Project:** MK173 | **Book:** Blue — *What Endures* | **Pass:** 1 (accepted plot skeleton)
**Setting:** Blue Realm; ancient engineered aquatic passage
**Arc purpose:** Demonstrate the cost of persistence without portraying past engineers as incompetent.

## Five accepted major plot points
1. **Vaelor introduces a continuously maintained passage between aquatic regions of differing orientation.**
2. **Aren identifies cumulative energetic, technical, archival, and relational obligations; repairs have increased interdependencies.**
3. **A scheduled repair would solve an immediate problem while adding a new dependency and reducing future adaptability.**
4. **Administrators choose a defensible, more resource-intensive alternative that avoids the most restrictive dependency, balancing current access and future flexibility.**
5. **Aren and Vaelor identify inherited engineering debt: generations of locally reasonable repairs can narrow future options; some inherited constraints remain essential safeguards.**

## Transition
Aren's findings prompt a substantive direct meeting with the Conservator.

## Narrative constraints
- No detailed scenes, chapter counts, exact dates, or dialogue are approved in this pass.
- Aren's relational atlas is an evolving research and cartographic framework, not five separate magical powers.
- Ancient network engineering is independent of municipal Genesis stabilization. Genesis storms, Domai, and Eldris are associated with Starscape singularity configuration and Genesis-field conditions, not network failure.
- Preserve existing AERA Transduction, velis, and realm-access constraints. Blue's Leviathans may visit White; chromatic natives do not freely traverse other chromatic realms.
- Treat precise performance, dates, institutional arrangements not explicitly accepted, and global failure forecasts as OPEN.
