# BLUE — Arc 01: The Ancient Passage
**Project:** MK173 | **Book:** Blue — *What Endures* | **Pass:** 1 (accepted plot skeleton)
**Setting:** Azure League, Sylvaris; ancient maritime Corridor
**Arc purpose:** Introduce long-duration engineering, maritime institutions, and Vaelor; move from configuration choice to persistence.

## Five accepted major plot points
1. **Aren travels from Serenwood to the Azure League aboard a timber-hulled, mastless, sigil-propelled ship, encountering large-scale intercontinental commerce.**
2. **The League's maritime economy and leadership—Lord Admiral Cassian Merrow and Trade Speaker Ilena Corvath—establish the political stakes of maintaining ancient infrastructure.**
3. **Aren investigates a centuries-old navigable maritime spatial Corridor belonging to the ancient weaving network, distinct from the vessel's own hull sigils.**
4. **Successive maintenance operations preserved passage but introduced dependencies and left original design rationales partly undocumented.**
5. **Vaelor, an independent Leviathan scholar of persistence and long-duration engineering, identifies a gap in Aren's ability to evaluate cumulative effects across generations.**

## Transition
Vaelor invites Aren to investigate Leviathan archival practices in the Blue Realm.

## Narrative constraints
- No detailed scenes, chapter counts, exact dates, or dialogue are approved in this pass.
- Aren's relational atlas is an evolving research and cartographic framework, not five separate magical powers.
- Ancient network engineering is independent of municipal Genesis stabilization. Genesis storms, Domai, and Eldris are associated with Starscape singularity configuration and Genesis-field conditions, not network failure.
- Preserve existing AERA Transduction, velis, and realm-access constraints. Blue's Leviathans may visit White; chromatic natives do not freely traverse other chromatic realms.
- Treat precise performance, dates, institutional arrangements not explicitly accepted, and global failure forecasts as OPEN.
