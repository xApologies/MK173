# BLUE — Arc 05: The Failing Network
**Project:** MK173 | **Book:** Blue — *What Endures* | **Pass:** 1 (accepted plot skeleton)
**Setting:** Azure League; Elarith; selected White-realm monitoring stations
**Arc purpose:** Establish a network-level deterioration pattern and the necessity of Violet's global geometry.

## Five accepted major plot points
1. **Aren, Vaelor, Seren, and the Directorate combine current measurements and historical records from multiple network regions under controlled oversight.**
2. **Comparative analysis reveals increasing coupling among subsystems formerly more independently maintainable, consistent with accumulated repairs and environmental evolution.**
3. **Forecasts show rising maintenance burdens and plausible localized or wider disruptions, without a reliable universal collapse deadline.**
4. **Aren and the Conservator agree that indefinite local correction is not an adequate assured strategy, but disagree about when intervention is justified; Seren raises cross-sovereign authorization.**
5. **Aren's atlas cannot yet reconstruct the network's full global non-ordinary connectivity; Violet Unicorn cartographic traditions and Aelira offer the next research direction, via Stormwall.**
