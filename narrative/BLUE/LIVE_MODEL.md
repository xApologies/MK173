## Arc index
01. **The Ancient Passage** — Introduce long-duration engineering, maritime institutions, and Vaelor; move from configuration choice to persistence.

03. **The Cost of Persistence** — Demonstrate the cost of persistence without portraying past engineers as incompetent.

05. **The Failing Network** — Establish a network-level deterioration pattern and the necessity of Violet's global geometry.

## Character continuity
- **Aren:** Cartographer and developing relational Weaver. Her fifth atlas advancement adds long-duration persistence, historical provenance, uncertainty, and accumulated dependencies.
- **Vaelor:** Independent Leviathan scholar; expert in archival continuity and long-duration infrastructure, not omniscient or a permanent traveling companion.
- **The Conservator:** Competent institutional custodian; past failed intervention informs justified caution. Conditional cooperation begins in Arc 04, disagreement persists.
- **Seren:** Directorate diplomatic/institutional liaison and the Conservator's daughter; coordinates research accountability and political participation.
- **Cassian Merrow / Ilena Corvath:** Azure League maritime and commercial leaders, respectively.

## Central causal line
An ancient maritime Corridor prompts inquiry into persistence. Leviathan archives show that preserving records is not preserving reasons. Maintenance decisions accumulate dependencies. The Conservator discloses an earlier failed transition. A joint network assessment reveals increasing inter-subsystem coupling, but the global geometric structure remains inadequately mapped.

## Non-negotiable separation of systems
- Network instability: environmental evolution interacting with accumulated engineering constraints.
- Genesis storms / Domai / Eldris: Starscape singularity and local Genesis-field conditions.
- Municipal stabilization arrays: independent from the ancient weaving network.
- Maritime propulsion: wooden mastless vessels, hull-integrated water-moving sigils; these are not the ancient spatial Corridors.

## Future dependencies
- Violet: Stormwall, Aelira, Unicorn cartography, global non-ordinary connectivity.
- White: intergovernmental authority and safe reconfiguration pathway; neither settled in Blue.
