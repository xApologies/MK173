# BLUE — Arc 02: The Leviathan’s Memory
**Project:** MK173 | **Book:** Blue — *What Endures* | **Pass:** 1 (accepted plot skeleton)
**Setting:** Blue Realm; Leviathan archival civilization
**Arc purpose:** Distinguish records from understanding and make historical uncertainty explicit.

## Five accepted major plot points
1. **Aren and Vaelor enter Blue's coherent but globally non-ordinary aquatic geometry, with multiple connected ocean orientations.**
2. **Leviathan archival institutions preserve engineering histories across generations; memory is institutional and fallible, not supernatural or perfect.**
3. **An ancient passage's records document interventions but omit some of their underlying reasons.**
4. **Competing generations of commentary show how interpretations harden into inherited engineering assumptions.**
5. **Aren and Vaelor cannot uniquely recover one lost design rationale; Aren adds provenance, uncertainty, and the separation of observations from interpretations to her atlas.**

## Transition
Turn from archival gaps to the mounting burden of keeping old infrastructure operational.

## Narrative constraints
- No detailed scenes, chapter counts, exact dates, or dialogue are approved in this pass.
- Aren's relational atlas is an evolving research and cartographic framework, not five separate magical powers.
- Ancient network engineering is independent of municipal Genesis stabilization. Genesis storms, Domai, and Eldris are associated with Starscape singularity configuration and Genesis-field conditions, not network failure.
- Preserve existing AERA Transduction, velis, and realm-access constraints. Blue's Leviathans may visit White; chromatic natives do not freely traverse other chromatic realms.
- Treat precise performance, dates, institutional arrangements not explicitly accepted, and global failure forecasts as OPEN.
