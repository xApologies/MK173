# Red — Arc 03: The Dragon’s Geometry

**Status:** ACCEPTED — Pass 1 narrative skeleton; detailed scene and chapter plans OPEN.
**Primary setting:** Asterhold; Red-realm gateway; Dragon civilization

## Main plot points

### 1. Research expedition
Elsen arranges a funded, documented professional visit through an established gateway to the Red realm.

### 2. Non-ordinary environment
Aren encounters locally navigable but globally non-ordinary Red geometry: opposing orientations, connected mountains, and geological arches. Ordinary surveys work locally but fail globally.

### 3. Tharos
Tharos, a Dragon Architect, initially suspects a familiar White-realm cartographic misunderstanding, then recognizes signs of engineered rather than natural continuity.

### 4. Connected surfaces
Tharos demonstrates Dragon architecture across local orientations; Aren learns to distinguish naturally unusual geometry from an artificially maintained relationship.

### 5. Historical evidence
Dragon records identify a class of engineered spatial constructions compatible with the mountain anomaly. Aren returns to White with a refined model; Tharos remains an independent collaborator in Red.

## Outcome
The mountain may involve maintained spatial engineering rather than an intrinsic geological oddity.

## Transition
Evidence warrants a formal Continuity Directorate review in Arc 04.
