# Red — Arc 04: The Conservator’s Intervention

**Status:** ACCEPTED — Pass 1 narrative skeleton; detailed scene and chapter plans OPEN.
**Primary setting:** Asterhold, Valedran Concord; Elarith Republic headquarters

## Main plot points

### 1. Formal referral
Elsen and Aren submit their findings and Tharos’s observations to the Continuity Directorate. The Conservator takes the evidence seriously.

### 2. Competing interpretations
Directorate review detects unusual stress but finds the network within established operational tolerances. The Conservator favors cautious maintenance; Aren suspects deeper accumulated constraints.

### 3. First meeting
The Conservator travels to Asterhold for a technical conference. Aren challenges assumptions of enduring stability; the Conservator questions whether her model accounts for accumulated engineering constraints. Both remain credible.

### 4. Restricted collaboration
Aren receives access to selected records and noncritical components, but not intrusive tests. Seren facilitates institutional liaison. Aren notices generations of individually reasonable maintenance corrections.

### 5. Institutional consequence
Aren advocates broader review; the Conservator authorizes noninvasive verification, not disruptive experiments. Asterhold civic authorities become involved.

## Outcome
Professional and philosophical disagreement begins; the Conservator is a responsible institutional antagonist, not an evil ruler.

## Transition
Historical multi-region archives enable Arc 05.
