# Red — Chapter Development Handoff

**Book:** Red — *The Threads That Bind*.
**Status:** Five Pass 1 arcs accepted; detailed arc expansions, chapter plans, and manuscripts not yet written.

## Accepted arc sequence
1. **The Impossible Mountain** — Aren's ordinary Asterhold life; incompatible independent surveys; expedition; mountain anomaly; verified contradiction and technical report.
2. **The First Weave** — Professional review; Architect demonstration; Aren's supervised first Weave; spatial-adjacency hypothesis.
3. **The Dragon's Geometry** — Authorized Red-realm visit; non-ordinary Dragon geometry; Tharos; natural versus engineered continuity; older construction evidence.
4. **The Conservator's Intervention** — Directorate review; first professional confrontation; restricted investigation; accumulated engineering constraints; institutional stakes.
5. **The Broken Map** — Historical atlas; globally incompatible ordinary mapping; maintenance correlations; Conservator reassessment; preliminary relational atlas and transition to Orange.

## Opening characters and setting
Aren (25, female cartographer at Asterhold Cartographic Survey); Professor Elsen, Mira, younger brother Tavin. Ordinary daily life includes an **abecca** shop. Asterhold's civic government is a council. Tharos retains an independent life in Red; the Conservator is competent, responsible, fallible, and not a conventional evil antagonist.

## Writing process
1. Reconcile accepted Red arcs with repository canon and World Clock.
2. Develop Red's arc-level expansions and cross-arc causality.
3. Establish Red-specific prose voice sheet and narrative treatment of mathematics.
4. Determine chapter counts and chapter-by-chapter outlines, beginning with Arc 01.
5. Draft one chapter at a time; audit chronology, terminology, realm access, mathematics, and provenance before committing.

## Guardrails
- Third-person limited primarily Aren; no premature knowledge of the network's true cause.
- Mathematical research matters socially and economically; do not overburden ordinary prose with equations.
- Municipal Genesis stabilization is independent of the ancient weaving network. Genesis storms, Domai, and Eldris originate in Starscape singularity/Genesis-field conditions, not network failure.
- Book color is ordinal, not a required basin identity. Preserve AERA rules and protected words: **Vaelith**, **vaen**, **Eln**, **Ahm**, **abecca**, **maege**, **maegi**, **velis**, **ru’ne**.
- Do not invent chapter count, detailed voice, exact dates, or legal patent regimes as accepted canon.
