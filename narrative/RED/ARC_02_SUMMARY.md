# Red — Arc 02: The First Weave

**Status:** ACCEPTED — Pass 1 narrative skeleton; detailed scene and chapter plans OPEN.
**Primary setting:** Asterhold, Valedran Concord

## Main plot points

### 1. Professional review
Aren’s report withstands scrutiny but lacks explanation. Similar anomalies appear in historical records near ancient infrastructure, though prior classifications are inconclusive.

### 2. Architect demonstration
A specialist Architect uses velis and admissible Weaving to establish a modest controlled structural relationship between distinct stone components.

### 3. First construction
Aren practices under supervision, fails at first, then succeeds in a small temporary relational Weave. She does not become an expert or cardsmith.

### 4. New hypothesis
Aren develops a preliminary spatial-adjacency model from the mountain records, but cannot reproduce or explain the full mechanism.

## Outcome
Aren gains a practical and conceptual introduction to Topological Weaving without inventing the discipline.

## Transition
Elsen arranges consultation with Tharos, a Dragon Architect familiar with large-scale connected geometry.
