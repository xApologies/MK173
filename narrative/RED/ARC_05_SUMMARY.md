# Red — Arc 05: The Broken Map

**Status:** ACCEPTED — Pass 1 narrative skeleton; detailed scene and chapter plans OPEN.
**Primary setting:** Asterhold; historical records from neighboring territories

## Main plot points

### 1. Historical atlas
Aren examines surveys before and after network maintenance: changes in river courses, travel distances and apparent settlement adjacency, each individually explainable.

### 2. Representation fails
A composite conventional atlas cannot preserve all independently verified relations. Aren realizes the map is incomplete, not necessarily false.

### 3. Accumulated corrections
Aren finds suggestive correlation between successive network changes and spatial inconsistencies, consistent with environmental evolution interacting with accumulated engineering constraints; she does not prove a complete global causal mechanism.

### 4. Conservator reassesses
The Conservator recognizes broader concern but prioritizes operational stability and permits noninvasive inquiry. Seren helps coordinate continued research.

### 5. First relational atlas
Aren constructs a preliminary atlas separating ordinary coordinates from engineered spatial relationships. Tharos reviews it. An unresolved living-system boundary question leads to Sunfall researchers and Vaelith collaboration.

## Outcome
Red resolves the initial representational puzzle, not the network’s ultimate stability or safe reconfiguration. No apocalyptic collapse occurs.
