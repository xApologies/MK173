# Red — Arc 01: The Impossible Mountain

**Status:** ACCEPTED — Pass 1 narrative skeleton; detailed scene and chapter plans OPEN.
**Primary setting:** Asterhold, Valedran Concord; Kaelmont Heights

## Main plot points

### 1. Ordinary life
Aren begins with abecca, work at Asterhold Cartographic Survey, and ordinary relationships with Mira, Professor Elsen, and younger brother Tavin. Show the city as a functioning, inhabited place.

### 2. Anomaly
Two independently prepared surveys identify the same mountain at incompatible geographic locations. Original observations are available.

### 3. Investigation
Aren checks coordinates, dates, markers and equipment records. Elsen authorizes a limited field survey; Mira assists logistics.

### 4. Discovery
At the frontier, Aren verifies the mountain and reproduces measurements revealing contradictory spatial relationships.

### 5. Consequence
Aren returns to Asterhold; both maps remain credible. She writes a technical report and realizes conventional cartography cannot adequately describe the observation.

## Outcome
Ordinary professional life becomes a reproducible mystery; not a solved network mechanism.

## Transition
Aren seeks a method to represent relationships, leading to Arc 02.
