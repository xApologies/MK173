# RED — Book Live Model v0.1

**Book:** Red — The Threads That Bind
**Status:** ACCEPTED Pass 1 five-arc narrative skeleton; Pass 2 arc expansion and chapters OPEN.
**Inheritance:** Existing MK173 repository and AERA framework. Do not duplicate their entire source corpora.

## Five-arc progression

01. **The Impossible Mountain** — Ordinary professional life becomes a reproducible mystery; not a solved network mechanism.
02. **The First Weave** — Aren gains a practical and conceptual introduction to Topological Weaving without inventing the discipline.
03. **The Dragon’s Geometry** — The mountain may involve maintained spatial engineering rather than an intrinsic geological oddity.
04. **The Conservator’s Intervention** — Professional and philosophical disagreement begins; the Conservator is a responsible institutional antagonist, not an evil ruler.
05. **The Broken Map** — Red resolves the initial representational puzzle, not the network’s ultimate stability or safe reconfiguration. No apocalyptic collapse occurs.

## Seven-book continuity handoff
- Red begins with an apparently impossible mountain, moves through a first Weave and Dragon geometric knowledge, and ends with a preliminary relational atlas.
- Orange opens with living-system boundary questions and Sunfall/Vaelith research.
- The Conservator is cautious and credible; Seren serves as an institutional liaison. Tharos retains an independent life in Red.
- No global network-collapse solution or proof is obtained in Red.
- Chromatic book colors identify book order, not mandatory basin alignment of events or characters.

## Canon safeguards
- The Starscape singularity configuration and local Genesis-field conditions govern Genesis storms, Domai and Eldris.
- The ancient engineered weaving network has a distinct instability mechanism: environmental evolution interacting with accumulated engineering constraints.
- Municipal stabilization is separate from the ancient network.
- Aren is a cartographer and developing Weaver, not a professional cardsmith.
- No arc-specific dates, travel durations, prices, scene counts or chapters are locked.
