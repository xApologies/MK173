# YELLOW — Arc 02: The Primordial Tradition

**Project:** MK173 — The Geometry of Existence  
**Book:** Yellow — The Shape of Becoming  
**Pass:** 1 — accepted narrative skeleton, not a chapter plan

## Setting and cast
- **Setting:** Dawnshore → Yellow Realm → Primordial architectural city
- **Characters:** Aren; Orun; Primordial architects; Lysara (correspondence)
- **Central conflict:** Aren must distinguish material, structural, functional and historical continuity rather than treating identity as a single invariant.

## Major plot points

### 1. Entering Yellow
Aren encounters locally coherent but globally non-ordinary landscapes with slow geological deformation and changing adjacency; natural transformation is not automatically failure.

### 2. Meeting Orun
Primordial Architect Orun asks what makes two configurations the same structure. Material provenance and uninterrupted history can persist when structural relationships do not.

### 3. City of successive forms
Primordial civic architecture and law distinguish material, structural, functional and historical continuity; some structures are recognized as ended despite retaining material.

### 4. Transformation archive
Intermediate states and their relationships matter. Identical final forms may have different histories; continuous history does not prove preservation of every property.

### 5. Admissibility question
Aren and Orun examine the Dawnshore passage records and agree to collaborate on which continuities matter; transformation is not necessarily reversible.

## Character and thematic progression
Orun becomes the third independent chromatic collaborator; identity becomes a question with several distinct meanings.
