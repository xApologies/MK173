# YELLOW — The Shape of Becoming

**MK173 Pass 1 Book Live Model v0.1**  
**Status:** All five arcs author-accepted; outline only, not manuscript.

## Book spine
Red: spatial relations beyond coordinates → Orange: evolving boundaries and continuity → **Yellow: transformation, identity, preservation criteria** → Green: competing admissible configurations and choice.

## Five-arc causal progression

### 01 — The Shifting Landscape
1. **Intercontinental journey:** After returning from Orange to Sunfall, Aren takes passage aboard a costly timber-hulled, mastless, sail-free merchant vessel driven by hull-integrated water-flow sigils. The crossing conveys the immense oceanic scale and economics of travel; no obligatory pirate attack.
2. **Arrival in Dawnshore:** Aren encounters a confederation of hereditary principalities and White-realm stone architecture with long histories of deliberate reconstruction; legal and civic identity may persist despite changed form.
3. **The commission:** Princess-Regent Lysara Auvell commissions Aren to study a major historic passage complex, providing legitimate access to records and engineers, not personal favoritism.
4. **Three histories:** The monumental stone passage complex underwent three major transformations, remaining operational while its geometry, components and connections changed. Red/Orange atlas methods cannot adequately characterize changed identity-defining relationships.
5. **Primordial introduction:** Dawnshore engineers identify Primordial architectural traditions as relevant; Lysara authorizes research exchange into Yellow. The arc ends with Aren preparing to enter the realm.

**Outcome:** From changing biological boundaries to transformation of the defining relationships themselves; a political commission expands Aren’s professional responsibilities.

### 02 — The Primordial Tradition
1. **Entering Yellow:** Aren encounters locally coherent but globally non-ordinary landscapes with slow geological deformation and changing adjacency; natural transformation is not automatically failure.
2. **Meeting Orun:** Primordial Architect Orun asks what makes two configurations the same structure. Material provenance and uninterrupted history can persist when structural relationships do not.
3. **City of successive forms:** Primordial civic architecture and law distinguish material, structural, functional and historical continuity; some structures are recognized as ended despite retaining material.
4. **Transformation archive:** Intermediate states and their relationships matter. Identical final forms may have different histories; continuous history does not prove preservation of every property.
5. **Admissibility question:** Aren and Orun examine the Dawnshore passage records and agree to collaborate on which continuities matter; transformation is not necessarily reversible.

**Outcome:** Orun becomes the third independent chromatic collaborator; identity becomes a question with several distinct meanings.

### 03 — Identity Through Transformation
1. **Transformation laboratory:** Prepared sigil arrays and calibrated instruments enable controlled experiments on stone architectural specimens under established AERA Transduction.
2. **Three transformations:** Test I rearranges the same components (material continuity); Test II replaces components but preserves selected function; Test III changes form and structural role, preserving only an uninterrupted history as applicable.
3. **Single-identity failure:** Aren’s attempt to treat all states as structurally and functionally equivalent fails conceptually; the evidence supports different continuities.
4. **Transformation atlas:** Aren and Orun extend the atlas to record successive configurations and explicitly test material, structural, functional and historical continuity; it does not settle all philosophical identity questions.
5. **Irreversibility:** A historical transformation remains stable and useful but cannot be reversed with available methods; documentation is not recoverability. They plan to revisit Dawnshore.

**Outcome:** Aren can describe what survives without presuming everything survives; no new universal magic mechanism is invented.

### 04 — The Fractured Structure
1. **Return and authorization:** Lysara authorizes a detailed investigation; Aren and Orun reconstruct successive engineering changes rather than presume the original design was superior.
2. **Lost redundancy:** A past transformation removed a relationship distributing loads across connected sections, replacing it with a functioning but narrower load-bearing configuration.
3. **Invisible fracture:** No visible crack, failed sigil or immediate collapse: the fracture is relational, distinguishing current stability from tolerance of changing conditions.
4. **Civic decision:** Lysara weighs monitoring, supplemental relationships and reconstruction; authorizes phased assessment and proportionate temporary restrictions rather than reckless rebuilding.
5. **Network analogy, not identity:** Aren recognizes how accumulated changes can conceal lost flexibility; the passage is independent of the ancient network and not evidence of the same exact failure mechanism. She sends findings to Elsen and the Directorate.

**Outcome:** Engineering choices become inherited constraints; Lysara becomes a trusted political contact and Orun remains an independent expert.

### 05 — What Remains
1. **Current-state inventory:** Aren and Orun record present operating relationships, compensating modifications and inherited dependencies; the structure is a product of its full history, not merely a damaged original.
2. **Contextual necessity:** Classify necessary relationships, redundancy and condition-dependent dependencies; essentiality is relative to specified functions and environments.
3. **Preservation test:** Compare existing configuration with three candidate modifications on a limited noncritical section: A preserves current function but adds dependency; B preserves function and improves tolerance to a specified change; C preserves material but fails an essential functional requirement. These are working narrative test outcomes, not derived equations.
4. **Third atlas:** Aren adds explicit preservation requirements to Red spatial relationships and Orange evolving continuity; Yellow tracks transformed properties. Several configurations may satisfy the same requirements without identifying a unique best choice.
5. **Decision and onward:** Lysara authorizes continued testing and monitoring, not full rebuilding. Orun returns to Yellow. Aren submits findings to Elsen and Directorate; Conservator identifies unresolved selection/safety problem. Academic links point to Serenwood Commonwealth and Green-realm researchers.

**Outcome:** Yellow ends with preservation criteria but no universal selection rule; Aren has greater respect for cautious institutional engineering.

## Major characters and relationships
- **Aren:** professional cartographer and developing Weaver; not automatically a cardsmith, alchemist or Primordial Architect. Her atlas gains transformation records and context-dependent preservation criteria.
- **Orun:** independent Primordial Architect; third chromatic collaborator after Tharos and Saelith. Professional expertise in transformations, preservation and irreversibility.
- **Princess-Regent Lysara Auvell:** Dawnshore commissioning authority; balances engineering evidence, public safety and political responsibility; ongoing professional relationship with Aren.
- **Professor Elsen:** academic correspondent and facilitator.
- **The Conservator / Seren:** Directorate correspondence and institutional assessment; no premature reconciliation or proof of network solution.

## Geographical and political movement
Sunfall Republic → sigil-propelled wooden maritime crossing → Dawnshore Principalities (Tyranor) → Yellow Realm → Dawnshore → research invitation toward Serenwood Commonwealth (Green). No exact voyage time, ship dimensions or political map coordinates established.

## Atlas progression
1. **Red:** represent engineered spatial relationships independently of conventional coordinates.
2. **Orange:** represent continuity across evolving living-system boundaries, subject to bounded admissible changes.
3. **Yellow:** distinguish material / structural / functional / historical continuity; record transformation histories, irreversible loss, and explicit preservation criteria relative to operating conditions.
4. **Open for Green:** how to choose among multiple admissible configurations and account for consequences.

## Canon guardrails
- Six chromatic realms (Red–Violet) and White superposition/material realm; no book color is a seventh chromatic energy basin.
- Dawnshore passage complex is independent of the ancient network; analogy is not a causal identification.
- Ancient network failure mechanism: environmental evolution interacting with accumulated engineering constraints.
- Starscape singularity / local Genesis-field conditions govern storms, Domai and Eldris; municipal stabilization is a separate system.
- The Primordial transformations and tests are narrative demonstrations, not new universal AERA operators or proven field equations.
- Realm travel restrictions and protected AERA terminology remain unchanged.
