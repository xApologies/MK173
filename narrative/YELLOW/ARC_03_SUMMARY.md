## Setting and cast
- **Setting:** Yellow Realm — Primordial architectural research complex
- **Characters:** Aren; Orun; Primordial research team
- **Central conflict:** Can a relational atlas track substantial transformations without asserting an unsupported universal identity?

## Major plot points

### 1. Transformation laboratory
Prepared sigil arrays and calibrated instruments enable controlled experiments on stone architectural specimens under established AERA Transduction.

### 2. Three transformations
Test I rearranges the same components (material continuity); Test II replaces components but preserves selected function; Test III changes form and structural role, preserving only an uninterrupted history as applicable.

### 3. Single-identity failure
Aren’s attempt to treat all states as structurally and functionally equivalent fails conceptually; the evidence supports different continuities.

### 4. Transformation atlas
Aren and Orun extend the atlas to record successive configurations and explicitly test material, structural, functional and historical continuity; it does not settle all philosophical identity questions.

### 5. Irreversibility
A historical transformation remains stable and useful but cannot be reversed with available methods; documentation is not recoverability. They plan to revisit Dawnshore.

## Character and thematic progression
Aren can describe what survives without presuming everything survives; no new universal magic mechanism is invented.
