# YELLOW — Arc 01: The Shifting Landscape

**Project:** MK173 — The Geometry of Existence  
**Book:** Yellow — The Shape of Becoming  
**Pass:** 1 — accepted narrative skeleton, not a chapter plan  
**Status:** AUTHOR-ACCEPTED (2026-10-24)

## Setting and cast
- **Setting:** Sunfall Republic → ocean crossing → Dawnshore Principalities (Tyranor)
- **Characters:** Aren; Professor Elsen (correspondence); Princess-Regent Lysara Auvell; Dawnshore engineers
- **Central conflict:** Aren’s relational atlas represents evolving boundaries but not transformations of the relationships that define a structure.

## Major plot points

### 1. Intercontinental journey
After returning from Orange to Sunfall, Aren takes passage aboard a costly timber-hulled, mastless, sail-free merchant vessel driven by hull-integrated water-flow sigils. The crossing conveys the immense oceanic scale and economics of travel; no obligatory pirate attack.

### 2. Arrival in Dawnshore
Aren encounters a confederation of hereditary principalities and White-realm stone architecture with long histories of deliberate reconstruction; legal and civic identity may persist despite changed form.

### 3. The commission
Princess-Regent Lysara Auvell commissions Aren to study a major historic passage complex, providing legitimate access to records and engineers, not personal favoritism.

### 4. Three histories
The monumental stone passage complex underwent three major transformations, remaining operational while its geometry, components and connections changed. Red/Orange atlas methods cannot adequately characterize changed identity-defining relationships.

### 5. Primordial introduction
Dawnshore engineers identify Primordial architectural traditions as relevant; Lysara authorizes research exchange into Yellow. The arc ends with Aren preparing to enter the realm.

## Character and thematic progression
From changing biological boundaries to transformation of the defining relationships themselves; a political commission expands Aren’s professional responsibilities.

## Transition
Yellow Arc 02: Aren enters the Yellow Realm and meets Orun.

## Scope and continuity
- Plot points are accepted at Pass 1; precise scene order, chapter counts, dialogue, dates, travel durations and technical formulae remain OPEN.
- The Dawnshore passage complex is a civic structure, **not** a component of the ancient weaving network.
- Ancient-network instability: environmental evolution interacting with accumulated engineering constraints.
- Genesis storms, Domai and Eldris arise from Starscape singularity / Genesis-field conditions, not from the ancient network.
- Municipal Genesis-field stabilization remains independent of the ancient network.
- Yellow is a chromatic realm with locally navigable, globally non-ordinary geometry; White remains the material realm.
- Protected terms: Vaelith, velis, vaen, ru’ne, maegi, abecca, eln, ahm.
