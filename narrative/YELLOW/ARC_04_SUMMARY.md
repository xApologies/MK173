# YELLOW — Arc 04: The Fractured Structure

**Project:** MK173 — The Geometry of Existence  
**Book:** Yellow — The Shape of Becoming  
**Pass:** 1 — accepted narrative skeleton, not a chapter plan  
**Status:** AUTHOR-ACCEPTED (2026-10-24)

## Setting and cast
- **Setting:** Dawnshore Principalities — historic passage complex
- **Characters:** Aren; Orun; Princess-Regent Lysara; engineering council; chief maintenance Architect
- **Central conflict:** A historic transformation preserved present function but removed an alternative structural relationship and reduced redundancy.

## Major plot points

### 1. Return and authorization
Lysara authorizes a detailed investigation; Aren and Orun reconstruct successive engineering changes rather than presume the original design was superior.

### 2. Lost redundancy
A past transformation removed a relationship distributing loads across connected sections, replacing it with a functioning but narrower load-bearing configuration.

### 3. Invisible fracture
No visible crack, failed sigil or immediate collapse: the fracture is relational, distinguishing current stability from tolerance of changing conditions.

### 4. Civic decision
Lysara weighs monitoring, supplemental relationships and reconstruction; authorizes phased assessment and proportionate temporary restrictions rather than reckless rebuilding.

### 5. Network analogy, not identity
Aren recognizes how accumulated changes can conceal lost flexibility; the passage is independent of the ancient network and not evidence of the same exact failure mechanism. She sends findings to Elsen and the Directorate.

## Character and thematic progression
Engineering choices become inherited constraints; Lysara becomes a trusted political contact and Orun remains an independent expert.
