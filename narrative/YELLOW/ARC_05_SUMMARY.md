## Setting and cast

- **Central conflict:** Which remaining relationships are essential under specified functions and conditions, and how should competing admissible futures be compared?

## Major plot points

### 1. Current-state inventory
Aren and Orun record present operating relationships, compensating modifications and inherited dependencies; the structure is a product of its full history, not merely a damaged original.

### 2. Contextual necessity
Classify necessary relationships, redundancy and condition-dependent dependencies; essentiality is relative to specified functions and environments.

### 3. Preservation test
Compare existing configuration with three candidate modifications on a limited noncritical section: A preserves current function but adds dependency; B preserves function and improves tolerance to a specified change; C preserves material but fails an essential functional requirement. These are working narrative test outcomes, not derived equations.

### 4. Third atlas
Aren adds explicit preservation requirements to Red spatial relationships and Orange evolving continuity; Yellow tracks transformed properties. Several configurations may satisfy the same requirements without identifying a unique best choice.

### 5. Decision and onward
Lysara authorizes continued testing and monitoring, not full rebuilding. Orun returns to Yellow. Aren submits findings to Elsen and Directorate; Conservator identifies unresolved selection/safety problem. Academic links point to Serenwood Commonwealth and Green-realm researchers.

## Character and thematic progression
Yellow ends with preservation criteria but no universal selection rule; Aren has greater respect for cautious institutional engineering.
