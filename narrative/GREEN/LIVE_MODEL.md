## Character and institutional continuity

- **Aren:** cartographer and developing relational Weaver; not a professional cardsmith. Increasingly respects technical and civic responsibilities.
- **Iri:** independent Sprite researcher; collaborator, not a hive-mind emissary or permanent traveling companion. Remains in Green to observe the Collective's implementation.
- **Pellian Vey:** Serenwood Research Commissioner; institutional sponsor of the initial research connection.
- **Sprite Collective:** civic/research body of autonomous persons, not a shared consciousness. Its final decision is a compromise made through legitimate procedures.
- **Conservator:** thematic counterpart; does not need direct appearance in Green. Neither his caution nor Aren's inquiry is categorically vindicated.

## Geography, magic and causal safeguards

- Serenwood is a **White-realm** commonwealth. Green's non-ordinary geometry exists in the **Green chromatic realm**; do not conflate them.
- Green remains an approximately 80% Green chromatic realm with a 20% mixture of other basins; locally coherent, globally non-ordinary geometry.
- Humans may visit all chromatic realms; native Sprite access is Green and White, not arbitrary other chromatic realms.
- A configuration-space atlas is a **mathematical representation**, not a new physical realm, chromatic basin, or magic system.
- Weaving is a specialization of AERA Transduction; velis, sigils and ru’ne inherit their existing AERA constraints. Do not invent unrestricted control or new physical laws.
- The ancient network's growing instability: **environmental evolution interacting with accumulated engineering constraints**. The Sprite case studies are not secretly network infrastructure.
- Municipal Genesis stabilization systems remain independent of the ancient weaving network.
- Genesis storms, Domai and Eldris concern Starscape singularity configuration/local Genesis-field conditions, **not** ancient-network failure or configuration trapping.
- No arc confers moral decision-making authority on the atlas. Endpoint admissibility does not prove a safe transition, reversibility, or long-duration persistence.
