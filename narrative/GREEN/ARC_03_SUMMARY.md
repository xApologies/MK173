# GREEN — Arc 03: The Unwritten Geometry

**Status:** ACCEPTED — Pass 1 narrative skeleton; not a chapter outline or manuscript.
**Book:** Green — The Unwritten Path (Book 4 of 7).
**Setting:** Green Realm, Sprite research collective.
**Participants:** Aren; Iri; Sprite research team.

## Principal plot points

### 1. Atlas of unrealized configurations
Researchers have numerous possible infrastructure states, most never constructed. Iri challenges Aren to map their relations rather than only realized places.

### 2. Configuration space
The team distinguishes admissible states from admissible transitions. Two individually valid states may lack a safe path between them; intermediate conditions matter.

### 3. Unreachable solution
An attractive proposed state meets endpoint requirements, but every currently known route passes through an intermediate violation of an essential preservation requirement.

### 4. The unwritten map
Aren extends the atlas to distinguish admissible configurations, admissible transitions, and states reachable from the current state. Reachability claims remain bounded by known constraints and transitions.

### 5. Incomplete knowledge
The Collective confronts untested transitions and missing dependencies. More research may help, but total certainty is unavailable and postponement has costs.

## Aren’s development
Aren distinguishes desirable destinations from safe, known paths to them; the map exposes uncertainty without eliminating judgment.

## Causal transition
An excluded historical configuration raises questions about institutional prohibition and reversibility.
