# GREEN — Arc 01: The Forest of Possibilities

**Status:** ACCEPTED — Pass 1 narrative skeleton; not a chapter outline or manuscript.
**Book:** Green — The Unwritten Path (Book 4 of 7).
**Setting:** Dawnshore Principalities → Serenwood Commonwealth, Tyranor.
**Participants:** Aren; Iri; Research Commissioner Pellian Vey; Professor Elsen (correspondence).

## Principal plot points

### 1. Invitation to Serenwood
Following the Dawnshore work, a Serenwood research institution invites Aren to study systems with multiple admissible configurations; Pellian Vey authorizes participation. Travel uses established terrestrial Corridors.

### 2. Commonwealth of interdependent communities
Serenwood is a parliamentary commonwealth of autonomous communities sharing transport and protected infrastructure. Its institutions negotiate competing legitimate interests; its political culture is not caused by Green energy.

### 3. Forest preserve with several valid paths
A protected White-realm forest preserve has multiple mutually incompatible proposals: better access, ecological preservation, or research expansion. Yellow-derived preservation tests admit several and cannot choose between them.

### 4. Meeting Iri
Iri, an independent Sprite researcher visiting White, studies recursive systems and configurations. Iri observes that Aren maps each alternative better than she maps relations among alternatives. They agree to collaborate.

### 5. First map of possibilities
Aren and Iri represent proposed engineering states and their dependencies; a choice can eliminate later options. They find alternatives with similar immediate results but different future flexibility, without selecting the final preserve design.

## Aren’s development
Aren learns that preserving present requirements does not determine which admissible future to choose, or what future options a choice forecloses.

## Causal transition
Iri invites Aren to Green to study more intricate recursive geometry and interdependent configurations.
