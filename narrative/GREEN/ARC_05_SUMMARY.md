### 2. Atlas of consequences
Aren and Iri compare present function, safe reachability, reversibility, preserved future options, social distribution of costs and benefits, and explicit uncertainty. The atlas is decision support, not an automatic moral chooser.

### 3. Aren and Iri disagree
Aren prioritizes future flexibility; Iri prioritizes present community needs. They agree on the technical evidence but weigh values differently.
