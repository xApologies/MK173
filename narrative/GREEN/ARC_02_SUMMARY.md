# GREEN — Arc 02: The Sprite Collective

**Status:** ACCEPTED — Pass 1 narrative skeleton; not a chapter outline or manuscript.
**Book:** Green — The Unwritten Path (Book 4 of 7).
**Setting:** Serenwood → Green Realm, Sprite settlement.
**Participants:** Aren; Iri; Sprite civic collective and researchers.

## Principal plot points

### 1. Entering the recursive realm
Through an established gateway, Aren visits Green, whose locally navigable but globally non-ordinary geometry contains nested forests, enormous trees, and interiors with unfamiliar effective scale.

### 2. Civilization within the forest
Iri introduces inhabited nested settlements, markets, homes, workshops, and cartographic traditions that link local representations rather than rely on one global scale.

### 3. Meaning of the Collective
A Sprite Collective is a civic/research institution coordinating independent individuals and communities, NOT a hive mind or shared consciousness. Participants disagree over legitimate interests.

### 4. A connection with cascading effects
A proposed passage between settlements improves access but redistributes advantages and constrains future routes across several communities. Local admissibility does not imply globally compatible choices.

### 5. First collective model
Aren and Iri model dependencies between communities and possible passage configurations; some individually valid choices cannot coexist. Mathematics does not choose whose interests prevail.

## Aren’s development
Aren learns that a local choice can recursively alter the options of distant dependent communities.

## Causal transition
The collaborators seek a formal geometry of admissible configurations and their transitions.
