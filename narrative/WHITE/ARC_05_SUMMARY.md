# WHITE — Arc 05: The World Rewritten

**Project:** MK173 | **Book:** White — *The Geometry of Existence* | **Pass:** 1 accepted narrative skeleton
**Primary setting:** Elarith → Valedran Concord → Asterhold → Kaelmont Heights

## Accepted major plot points

### 1. A world beginning to change
The phased program continues with measurable improvements in some regions and unresolved constraints elsewhere; no total permanent repair is claimed.

### 2. Inheritance of responsibility
Governments establish continuing oversight, maintenance standards, documentation of rationale and uncertainty, independent review, and explicit transition safeguards. Seren helps coordinate; the Conservator remains in stewardship.

### 3. Six paths continue
Tharos continues structural-continuity work; Saelith adaptive living engineering; Orun transformation records; Iri future-option research; Vaelor archival provenance; Aelira global cartography. Each retains their own life and correspondence with Aren.

### 4. Return to Asterhold
Aren reunites with Professor Elsen, Mira and her younger brother Tavin, revisits the Survey and her familiar abecca shop; ordinary life supplies the meaning of infrastructure.

### 5. Impossible mountain revisited
At Kaelmont Heights, the mountain remains physically present; the two original surveys are understood through compatible local charts and engineered relationships rather than erased or declared false.

### 6. The final map
Aren submits her evolving relational atlas as shared scholarly knowledge, returns to her abecca table and opens a blank notebook. Proposed closing line: “The world had never been impossible. She had only needed a better map.”

## Arc outcome / transition
The saga closes with Aren still a cartographer, the Conservator still responsible, and discovery remaining open.

## Scope and continuity
- Plot-level summary only. No chapter count, scene order, exact chronology, dialogue, or full mathematical proof is locked.
- The network’s instability arises from environmental evolution interacting with accumulated engineering constraints.
- Municipal Genesis-field stabilization remains independent of the ancient weaving network. Genesis storms, Domai and Eldris relate to Starscape singularity and Genesis-field conditions, not network deterioration.
- The six chromatic collaborators can meet in White, not in each other’s chromatic realms. Sixfold means complementary expertise, not a new magic system.
- Every tested transition remains constrained by AERA Transduction and existing technical, political, and realm-access rules.
