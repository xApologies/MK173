# WHITE — Arc 02: The Gathering of Six

**Project:** MK173 | **Book:** White — *The Geometry of Existence* | **Pass:** 1 accepted narrative skeleton
**Primary setting:** Elarith Directorate research facilities

## Accepted major plot points

### 1. The reunion
Tharos, Saelith, Orun, Iri, Vaelor and Aelira gather in White with independent professional obligations; their reunion illustrates Aren’s growth.

### 2. Sixfold engineering review
Tharos checks spatial continuity; Saelith adaptation; Orun preserved properties; Iri reachability and options; Vaelor persistence and provenance; Aelira global compatibility. Aren integrates findings; the Conservator contributes operational constraints.

### 3. The transition that almost works
A promising candidate reduces accumulated dependencies but requires a vulnerable intermediate arrangement where essential relationships depend on insufficiently protected temporary support.

### 4. The controlled transition proposal
A phased, bounded test with independent monitoring and termination conditions is proposed. Each intermediate configuration needs separate verification; gradual does not automatically mean safe.

### 5. The authorization dispute
The Conservator withholds technical certification over an unbounded disengagement risk; the oversight body defers approval. Seren maintains the distinction between certification and governmental authorization.

## Arc outcome / transition
The Conservator’s refusal creates a precise unresolved safety requirement for Arc 03.

## Scope and continuity
- Plot-level summary only. No chapter count, scene order, exact chronology, dialogue, or full mathematical proof is locked.
- The network’s instability arises from environmental evolution interacting with accumulated engineering constraints.
- Municipal Genesis-field stabilization remains independent of the ancient weaving network. Genesis storms, Domai and Eldris relate to Starscape singularity and Genesis-field conditions, not network deterioration.
- The six chromatic collaborators can meet in White, not in each other’s chromatic realms. Sixfold means complementary expertise, not a new magic system.
- Every tested transition remains constrained by AERA Transduction and existing technical, political, and realm-access rules.
