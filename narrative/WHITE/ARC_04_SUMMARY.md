# WHITE — Arc 04: The Geometry of Choice

**Project:** MK173 | **Book:** White — *The Geometry of Existence* | **Pass:** 1 accepted narrative skeleton
**Primary setting:** Elarith; bounded test subsystem; international oversight

## Accepted major plot points

### 1. Architecture of overlap
Establish and verify a new relationship before retiring the old one; explicitly check that overlap is mutually admissible and globally compatible, not magical duplication.

### 2. Sixfold verification
The collaborators test each intermediate stage against continuity, adaptation, transformation, reachability, persistence, global compatibility and Directorate operating requirements; invalid stages are revised.

### 3. First successful transition
An authorized noncritical subsystem test successfully verifies new support, maintains essential functions, and disengages the old relationship; it proves only bounded feasibility.

### 4. Decision of governments
The assembly weighs costs, services, recovery and liability, then authorizes a phased reconfiguration program with regional consent, monitoring and suspension conditions.

### 5. Conservator’s certification
The Conservator reviews documented residual uncertainties and signs technical certification because the revised safety case addresses his concrete objections, not because risk disappears.

### 6. First regional stage
A monitored coordinated regional reconfiguration succeeds, reduces selected dependencies and restores some independent manageability; remaining stages are not presumed safe.

## Arc outcome / transition
The intellectual, institutional and personal climax is achieved; the long-term consequences remain for Arc 05.

## Scope and continuity
- Plot-level summary only. No chapter count, scene order, exact chronology, dialogue, or full mathematical proof is locked.
- The network’s instability arises from environmental evolution interacting with accumulated engineering constraints.
- Municipal Genesis-field stabilization remains independent of the ancient weaving network. Genesis storms, Domai and Eldris relate to Starscape singularity and Genesis-field conditions, not network deterioration.
- The six chromatic collaborators can meet in White, not in each other’s chromatic realms. Sixfold means complementary expertise, not a new magic system.
- Every tested transition remains constrained by AERA Transduction and existing technical, political, and realm-access rules.
