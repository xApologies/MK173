## Narrative spine
White begins with a legitimate international research accord, proceeds through sixfold engineering review and the Conservator’s refusal to certify a vulnerable transition, resolves their opposition through shared responsibility, tests an overlapping-transition approach, secures sovereign authorization, and ends with a phased implementation and Aren’s homecoming.

## Character outcomes
- **Aren:** remains a cartographer and developing Weaver, not a supreme authority or omnipotent engineer. Her relational atlas integrates the research of Red through Violet; she shares it and continues exploring.
- **The Conservator:** recognizes that preserving essential functions may require changing inherited configuration; signs a revised, adequately supported technical certification and remains a responsible steward.
- **Seren:** institutional and diplomatic coordinator; preserves representation, accountability and oversight independently of her familial relationship.
- **Professor Elsen, Mira, Tavin:** Aren’s stable home relationships recur at Asterhold; Tavin is her younger brother.
- **Tharos, Saelith, Orun, Iri, Vaelor, Aelira:** six independent chromatic collaborators, not six mandatory power components, continue their distinct professional lives.

## End state
The ancient network has begun monitored, phased reconfiguration. It is not completely or permanently fixed. Municipal stabilization remains independent. The impossible mountain remains; its seemingly contradictory surveys become intelligible under the completed relational atlas. Aren returns to abecca and opens a new notebook.
