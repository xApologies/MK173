# WHITE — Arc 01: The Return to White

**Project:** MK173 | **Book:** White — *The Geometry of Existence* | **Pass:** 1 accepted narrative skeleton
**Primary setting:** Stormwall → Elarith Republic

## Accepted major plot points

### 1. Leaving Stormwall
Aren departs with Seren and Directorate personnel, reviews the impossible-mountain report, and recognizes that the sixfold framework is not yet a safe transition plan.

### 2. The capital of responsibility
Elarith’s civic and Directorate institutions host the international investigation; Aren enters as a visiting researcher rather than sovereign decision-maker.

### 3. The assembly of governments
President Celene Arveth convenes participating governments; Minister Oren Valcrest coordinates diplomacy; Aren presents the mathematical findings, the Conservator the operational assessment, and Seren the institutional implications.

### 4. The question of authority
Delegates debate sovereignty, risk, liability, affected communities and the limits of Directorate authority. No researcher or ruler can impose global reconfiguration unilaterally.

### 5. The provisional accord
The Elarith Research Accord authorizes shared investigation and explicitly reviewed tests, not immediate global rebuilding. The Directorate monitors safety, governments retain sovereignty, and six chromatic experts are invited to work in White.

## Arc outcome / transition
Political legitimacy becomes an explicit engineering requirement; the research program can now assemble in Elarith.

## Scope and continuity
- Plot-level summary only. No chapter count, scene order, exact chronology, dialogue, or full mathematical proof is locked.
- The network’s instability arises from environmental evolution interacting with accumulated engineering constraints.
- Municipal Genesis-field stabilization remains independent of the ancient weaving network. Genesis storms, Domai and Eldris relate to Starscape singularity and Genesis-field conditions, not network deterioration.
- The six chromatic collaborators can meet in White, not in each other’s chromatic realms. Sixfold means complementary expertise, not a new magic system.
- Every tested transition remains constrained by AERA Transduction and existing technical, political, and realm-access rules.
