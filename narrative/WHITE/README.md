The Elarith research accord follows Violet's Stormwall synthesis. The six experts reunite in White; a vulnerable candidate is refused, redesigned and subjected to bounded testing. Sovereign authorization and revised technical certification support a monitored regional stage. The ending continues phased work with unresolved constraints and returns Aren to Asterhold and the still-present Kaelmont mountain. The original surveys become intelligible; they are not erased.

Sixfold collaboration combines expertise within existing AERA rules. Municipal stabilization and Starscape-driven environmental phenomena remain separate from ancient-network deterioration. The network is not completely or permanently fixed. Each collaborator retains an independent life; Aren remains a cartographer and developing Weaver, and the Conservator remains a responsible steward.

The 27 major points, including six each in Arcs 04 and 05, are preserved as supplied. The closing line remains proposed. Chronology, technical proof, signatories, procedural detail, chapters, scenes and dialogue remain OPEN. White does not explicitly settle the earlier Seren parentage ambiguity.
