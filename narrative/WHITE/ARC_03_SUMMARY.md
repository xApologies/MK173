# WHITE — Arc 03: The Conservator’s Last Stand

**Project:** MK173 | **Book:** White — *The Geometry of Existence* | **Pass:** 1 accepted narrative skeleton
**Primary setting:** Elarith Directorate, oversight assembly, monitored subsystem

## Accepted major plot points

### 1. The refusal
Despite improved analysis, the Conservator declines to certify a transition whose temporary support might be impossible to disengage or recover from under an unknown dependency.

### 2. The cost of waiting
Aren and Vaelor show that continued maintenance also risks shrinking future safe pathways, without claiming imminent collapse.

### 3. The record of the first failure
Aren and the Conservator revisit the earlier intervention that disrupted transport due to an unrecognized intermediate dependency; both acknowledge improved methods and remaining uncertainty.

### 4. The last stand
Before the assembly, the Conservator admits the network must eventually change but insists on a credible recovery strategy. Aren withdraws the premature certification request; he agrees to help redesign the plan.

### 5. Another path
The team investigates overlapping, independently verified relationships to avoid transferring all essential functions through one vulnerable intermediate state; the idea remains unproven.

## Arc outcome / transition
Personal opposition resolves into shared engineering responsibility, enabling Arc 04.
