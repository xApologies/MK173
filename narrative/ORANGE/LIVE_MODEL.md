## Mechanical boundaries

- White is superposition; Orange realm is approximately 80% Orange basin with other basins composing remaining 20%; non-ordinary geometry, locally coherent navigation.
- No new energy basin, universal transduction interface or effortless indefinite adaptive Weave is introduced.
- Trial and civic pilot are bounded; termination, monitoring, training, certification and energy requirements matter.
- Network instability follows environmental evolution interacting with accumulated engineering constraints; it is not caused by Genesis storms, Domai or Eldris. Municipal stabilizers are independent.
- Living-system analogy does not prove the ancient network can be safely reconfigured.
