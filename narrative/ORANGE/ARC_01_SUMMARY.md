# Orange — Arc 01: The Living Threshold

**Status:** ACCEPTED — Pass 1 narrative skeleton (not chapter plan)
**Setting:** Asterhold → Sunfall Republic → preparation for Orange Realm
**Characters:** Aren; Professor Elsen; Saelith; Sunfall scholars

## Major plot points

### 1. Invitation from Sunfall
Several weeks after Red, researchers studying long-term Genesis-engineered living structures invite Aren through Professor Elsen. Some engineered relationships require increasing adjustment as living systems develop; this is a research opportunity, not an emergency.

### 2. Sunfall research culture
Aren encounters a White-realm civilization studying evolving biological structures, botanical public spaces and Genesis-assisted agriculture, medicine and construction. Sunfall is not itself a Vaelith settlement, though its researchers collaborate with Orange.

### 3. The boundary that grows
A healthy experimental living structure has developed beyond its original engineered geometric boundary. Aren’s Red atlas describes the initial configuration but not the evolving boundary. The discrepancy concerns changing relationships, not biological failure.

### 4. Meeting Saelith
Saelith, an independent Vaelith researcher of biological coherence and relational engineering, explains that identity can persist despite changing material organization. Aren’s cartographic expertise offers complementary spatial representation.

### 5. Invitation across the threshold
Aren and Saelith produce a preliminary distinction between persistent identity and changing boundary. To observe naturally distributed coherence, Saelith arranges a professional visit to Orange; Aren accepts.

## Arc outcome
An atlas built for maintained spatial connections is inadequate for developing living boundaries.

## Transition
Aren travels to Orange with Saelith; she has not solved biological identity or the ancient network.
