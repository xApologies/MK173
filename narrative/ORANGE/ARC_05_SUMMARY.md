# Orange — Arc 05: The Living Boundary

**Status:** ACCEPTED — Pass 1 narrative skeleton (not chapter plan)
**Setting:** Orange Realm: living archive and research site → Sunfall Republic; preparation for Dawnshore
**Characters:** Aren; Saelith; Vaelith research team; Professor Elsen; Seren by correspondence

## Major plot points

### 1. Archive of changing boundaries
Generational records show living structures expanding, reorganizing and replacing material while retaining recognizable identities and functions. Their original engineered boundaries no longer match present form.

### 2. Persistent identity distinguished
Aren and Saelith separate material composition, present geometry and continuity through time. A snapshot alone cannot identify all continuity-defining relationships.

### 3. Boundary experiment
Under bounded conditions, the team tracks a developing living structure with a relational Weave and observation model. Its boundary and material organization change while measured identity-defining relationships persist; no universal theory is claimed.

### 4. Second relational atlas
Aren expands her Red atlas to represent evolving boundaries and continuity relationships through time. It still requires knowing which relationships actually define identity and does not establish safe network reconfiguration.

### 5. Question of transformation
A visiting scholar asks what happens when transformation changes the relationships by which identity was recognized. Primordial engineering may help. Through Sunfall and Professor Elsen, Aren arranges a Dawnshore Principalities introduction; Saelith stays to continue independent research.

## Arc outcome
Continuity through bounded change can be mapped; identity through deeper transformation remains unresolved.

## Transition
Yellow / The Shifting Landscape: Dawnshore Principalities and eventual Primordial collaboration.
