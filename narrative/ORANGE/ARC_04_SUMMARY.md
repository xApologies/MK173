# Orange — Arc 04: The Cost of Connection

**Status:** ACCEPTED — Pass 1 narrative skeleton (not chapter plan)
**Setting:** Orange Realm: civic bridge, engineering council and limited pilot area
**Characters:** Aren; Saelith; Vaelith engineering council; bridge users

## Major plot points

### 1. Public implementation proposal
The team seeks approval to replace or supplement the living bridge’s original maintained configuration. The existing bridge works, so the council requests risk, benefit and long-term maintenance analysis.

### 2. People who depend on it
Aren observes merchants, workers, families and maintenance specialists relying on the bridge. Closure, failure and future upkeep affect lives and trade.

### 3. Responsibility debated
Council members disagree in good faith: change may reduce growing maintenance burdens, but untested long-term adaptation has risks. Aren recognizes the Conservator’s legitimate responsibility without conceding her broader hypothesis.

### 4. Limited implementation
A council-authorized isolated pilot succeeds under monitoring and predefined stop/reversion conditions. It reveals the need for new maintenance training and institutional capacity.

### 5. Cost of preserving connection
Full conversion is deferred pending certification and staffing; continued research is authorized. Aren and Saelith document feasibility separately from operational safety and civic authorization; findings are shared with Professor Elsen.

## Arc outcome
Technical success does not confer authority to modify public infrastructure.

## Transition
Aren returns to the living archive to extend her atlas rather than attempt a premature network-wide solution.
