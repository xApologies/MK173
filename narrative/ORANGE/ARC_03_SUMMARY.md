# Orange — Arc 03: The Symbiotic Weave

**Status:** ACCEPTED — Pass 1 narrative skeleton (not chapter plan)
**Setting:** Orange Realm: living bridge, laboratory and civic engineering facilities
**Characters:** Aren; Saelith; Vaelith research team

## Major plot points

### 1. Living bridge problem
A public living bridge connects two city districts. It remains functional, but its fixed maintained Weave increasingly constrains natural development and demands repeated intervention.

### 2. Adaptive design
Aren models relational structure; Saelith identifies biological growth and identity constraints. They design a bounded adaptive Weave using existing velis anchoring, admissible relationships and controlled Transduction—not a new magic system.

### 3. Controlled trial fails safely
A smaller experimental structure accommodates changes inside the specified range but loses coherence beyond it. Researchers terminate the test. The issue is missing invariants, not merely insufficient energy.

### 4. Successful revised trial
The team distinguishes essential structural invariants from admissible biological variation; progressively tested Weave succeeds within specified bounds. It still needs energy, monitoring, maintenance and oversight.

### 5. Consequences of adaptation
Engineering authorities recognize a promising technique but require certification and long-duration testing before full public use. Saelith cautions that the ancient network is not equivalent to a bounded living bridge.

## Arc outcome
Adaptation needs both permitted changes and invariant relationships.

## Transition
Successful limited research prompts a civic decision on whether to deploy the new Weave.

## Boundaries
- The events above are accepted at the causal-outline level; exact dates, named local officials, scene order and dialogue remain OPEN.
