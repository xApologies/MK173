# Orange — Arc 02: The Vaelith

**Status:** ACCEPTED — Pass 1 narrative skeleton (not chapter plan)
**Setting:** Orange Realm: non-ordinary landscapes, Vaelith research city, living archive
**Characters:** Aren; Saelith; Vaelith scholars and civic representatives

## Major plot points

### 1. Crossing into Orange
Aren crosses an established Portal with Saelith. Human White natives can enter Orange; Vaelith may visit Orange and White, not other chromatic realms. Living forests and spatial surfaces connect across unfamiliar orientations while remaining locally navigable.

### 2. The city that grows
Vaelith buildings and bridges can be living structures that expand, renew and reorganize while retaining civic identity. Conventional mineral construction also exists. The city is an actual functioning civilization, not one organism.

### 3. Boundaries of individuality
Aren learns that interconnected biological relationships do not erase Vaelith autonomy or make all inhabitants a collective consciousness. Saelith’s independent colleagues, responsibilities and disagreements become visible.

### 4. Scholarly disagreement
Researchers debate preserving original engineered relationships versus revising them to accommodate ongoing growth. Both sides have legitimate safety and continuity concerns; Aren recognizes parallels with the Conservator.

### 5. Living archive
Records of successive living structures demonstrate that continuity sometimes requires controlled reconfiguration rather than freezing an original material arrangement. The analogy does not prove that the ancient network can be altered safely.

## Arc outcome
Continuity of identity may require changes in configuration.

## Transition
Aren and Saelith identify a bounded public engineering problem suitable for joint research.
