# MK173 — Mathematics and Prose Style Addendum

Status: WORKING proposal for incorporation into the accepted Narrative Constitution; no changes to AERA foundational mathematics.

## Accepted narrative foundation (existing repository)
- Adult high fantasy; **simple, elegant, sophisticated**.
- Primary third-person-limited viewpoint through Aren, with occasional other viewpoints when justified.
- Experience and character stakes precede technical exposition; mathematics governs reality without becoming a lecture.
- Arc-first design, five arcs per book; chapter structure remains open.

## Newly agreed direction
**Mathematics is foregrounded in civilization, selectively foregrounded in prose.** Genesis-field Transduction, sigils, ru’ne, cards, spatial locking, material growth, and relational Weaving make mathematics a significant economic, technical, professional, and political force. In research and discovery scenes, genuine mathematical reasoning should be visible, with explicit notation when meaningful and supported by AERA. In ordinary scenes, the mathematical infrastructure may remain implicit in how people live.

Noble houses, universities, guilds, governments, and commercial sponsors may finance mathematical teams developing valuable sigil configurations, Transduction applications, and licensed engineering methods. This is an endorsed worldbuilding direction, NOT a claim that a detailed patent statute, particular noble-house research monopoly, or global intellectual-property treaty has been accepted.

## Working examples, not newly locked canon
- Research domains: stone-growth arrays, structural spatial locking, hull-integrated hydrodynamic sigils, biological Weaving, Genesis cards, relational cartography.
- Possible commercial mechanisms: patents on implemented arrays, licenses, exclusive manufacture, contractual research sponsorship, industrial espionage, and disputes over public infrastructure.
- Distinguish an abstract mathematical truth from a specific proprietary engineered implementation; the legal distinction remains OPEN.
- Illustrative prose balance discussed: 60% character/environment/action, 30% reasoning/technical dialogue, 10% notation/formal demonstration. **Not a quota or required allocation.**

## Open decisions
- International versus jurisdictional patent recognition and enforcement.
- Detailed voice sheets, dialogue styles, and the degree of lyricism.
- Exact chapter counts and scene breakdowns.
- Whether and how intellectual-property disputes affect Red's plot; do not silently add major new plot events.
