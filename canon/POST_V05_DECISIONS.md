## Currency and economy — ACCEPTED
- Protected names: **eln** (E-L-N) banknotes; **ahm** (A-H-M) coins; **abecca** (A-B-E-C-C-A); **vaen**, not BAEN. Preserve Vaelith, velis, ru’ne, maege, maegi.
- **180 ahm = 1 eln**. Six ordinary chromatic denominations for **each** of coins and banknotes: Red 1, Orange 6, Yellow 12, Green 18, Blue 24, Violet 30. NOT consecutive 1–30 values or color bands.
- Six high-value White **eln banknotes**: 180, 360, 540, 720, 900, 1080 eln. No White ahm coin. '10,080' was not adopted over the illustrated 1,080.
- Ahm coin materials: Red iron+diamond; Orange copper+diamond; Yellow tin+diamond; Green nickel+diamond; Blue silver+diamond; Violet gold+diamond. Coin diamond is not necessarily a charged Genesis Diamond chip.
- Inherit MK163's economic benchmark schedules for Valedran, including six chromatic socioeconomic districts Red–Violet and White civic. Do not copy Kaira's personal purchases, MK163's world chronology, or political map.
- Six chromatic realms each support a broadly accepted **realm trade currency**, plus possible local sovereign currencies; this is not a single Starscape-wide cash currency. Working names: Talon, Vela, Orin, Lira, Tide, Astra. **Their denominations, 180:1 ratios, and exchange rates are NOT accepted**; do not project Valedran monetary values onto other realms.

## Land, materials, culture — ACCEPTED
- Protected noble estates within city stabilization territories may farm, lease vineyards, graze livestock, or manage timber plantations. City stabilization is civic infrastructure, not automatically controlled by the noble landowner.
- Eldris/Genesis hazards make open-Wilds livestock and logging hazardous. Meat is a premium food; ordinary diets are predominantly plant-based. Noble forestry makes wood moderately available but more expensive than grown stone.
- Sigil arrays grow ordinary Type 1 materials including geometrically precise stone blocks. Modular stone and spatially locked coherent assemblies are dominant ordinary and monumental construction. Wooden buildings and elaborate timber interiors are prestigious luxuries. Advanced alchemy Types 2–5 remain specialized.
- Large metal growth may be possible, but large-scale handling, joining, lifting, fabrication and industrial shipyard capacity are not automatically present in a romanticized pre-industrial society.
- Abecca culture, alchemy as material science, alchemical beverages and medicinal preparations available; exact recipes/effects open. Agricultural inheritance 17 slices x 13 crops; do not invent the unverified 221-crop list.

## Transportation and professional identity — ACCEPTED
- Principal MK173 ships: **timber hulls, no sails**, hull-integrated sigils that manipulate water flow around the vessel to propel it. No precise size/speed/energy mechanism approved. Timber ships are high-value capital goods.
- Convoys, naval fleets, giant marine creatures, intelligent visiting Leviathans, pirates, bandits, cartels and black markets are available world elements. No required evil world-conquest cabal of maegi.
- Aren is a professional cartographer specializing in general relational Topological Weaving, **not a professional cardsmith**. She can use prepared cards and design weaving configurations; physical card manufacture requires separate cardsmith expertise.
