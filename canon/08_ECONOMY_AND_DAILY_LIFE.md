# MK173 — Development 08: Economy and Daily Life
Source: Genesis/Astraeus workshop, 2026-10-24. Status: mixed; distinctions below control. MK173-specific, not AERA-wide law.

## ACCEPTED
- Valedran Concord uses **eln** and **ahm** for wages, rent, tax and ordinary trade. Exact conversion OPEN; do not import obsolete MK163 ratios.
- Sovereign governments across the White planet can issue regional currencies or share a monetary system. No universal everyday world currency; neither every city nor every continent requires a separate currency.
- Interregional and interworld commerce may use a common **accounting and settlement reference** without physical universal coins. Exact institutional arrangements and exchange rates OPEN.
- Six chromatic realms have distinct economic traditions. A dominant realm-wide monetary convention with locally issued currencies is a WORKING possibility, not a fixed rule.
- **Genesis Crystals, Genesis Dust and vaen** are specialized tradable resources, not automatically circulating money or a universal currency backing. `baen` / `BAEN` / `B-A-E-N` was an author-confirmed transcription error for **vaen (V-A-E-N)**; no separate BAEN substance exists.
- Operational Genesis cards can also be collected as mathematical art without activation; AERA's active card basin exclusivity still applies.

## DAILY LIFE AND PRODUCTION
Aren is a salaried Asterhold Cartographic Survey professional, financially independent but not rich. She can afford a modest apartment, abecca, ordinary equipment and some prepared cards; expensive collector cards and remote expeditions require saving or sponsorship. Illustrative monthly budget: housing 28%, food/household 18%, transport/equipment 15%, savings 15%, leisure/personal 12%, other 12% (WORKING, not a price law).

Regional sectors: Genesis-supported agriculture and raw materials; grown stone/glass, maege-glass, cardsmithing and precision craft; education, helkir, Architects, surveyors and research; caravans, shipping, banking and gateway services. Growth requires resources, skill and maintenance. Protected city clusters have lower risk/cost than Wilds crossings. White acts as an inter-realm trade hub under asymmetric native travel restrictions; it does not imply human monopoly.
