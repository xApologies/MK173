# MK173 world and story
Aren, cartographer in Asterhold, investigates reports of the same mountain in two geographically incompatible locations. Single physical mountain, engineered adjacency, no matter duplication. Conservator maintains inherited network whose repairs compound environmental mismatch; sudden shutdown dangerous, reconfiguration might preserve essential functions. City field stabilizers independent. Exact math OPEN.
World: White home planet ~80k-mile circumference; 20–30k-foot mountains; enormous trees, rivers and lakes. Protected 3–5-city networks amid vast Wilds. Original geography art: Aerdan north (Northern Crown, Valedran Concord), Tyranor east, Kaelith west, Thaldor south, Sylvaris archipelago. Four continents + archipelago depicted, earlier five-continent proposal unresolved. Political map's territory names/borders illustrative, not ratified. White cities late-17th/early-18th-century aesthetic with advanced Genesis engineering. Red Dragon citadels; Orange Vaelith living structures; Yellow Primordial mutable monoliths; Green Sprite nested settlements; Blue Leviathan colossal persistent works; Violet Unicorn adjacency cities. All chromatic realms have nonordinary geometry, not exclusive geometry types. Chromatic realm bridges connect White-domain worlds. Chronology conversion factors OPEN.
Politics: Asterhold civic council, Valedran Concord constitutional aristocracy, six chromatic hereditary noble ranks plus White landless civil noble rank; planetary kingdoms, republics, principalities and leagues. Economy: Concord eln/ahm; other states/realms may issue own currency; Genesis crystals, dust and vaen are specialized resources not universal coins. Operational Genesis cards may also be collected as geometric art.
