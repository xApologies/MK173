# MK173 — Development 10: Narrative Constitution
Source: Genesis/Astraeus workshop, 2026-10-24.

## ACCEPTED
- Independent AERA seven-book saga **The Geometry of Existence**, book colors Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Violet, White (ordinal, not basin rank).
- **Adult high fantasy** with selected philosophical/literary elements; mystery, adventure, exploration, politics, consequential but restrained violence. Not binary good-versus-evil or obligatory chosen-one Black Prophecy.
- Style: **simple, elegant, sophisticated**. Readers experience phenomena and character stakes before technical exposition; mathematics governs reality without becoming a lecture.
- Arc-first design, **five major arcs per book** as working baseline (35 total); arcs are narrative transitions, chapters organized beneath them. No padding to a quota.
- Aren, 25-year-old female cartographer, central third-person-limited viewpoint. Conservator is a responsible but fallible custodian antagonist, not inherently evil. Supporting cast has independent lives and realm-access restrictions.
- AERA source authority: six chromatic basins, White superposition, Conceive→Shape→Hold→Resolve→Manifest→Release, velis, ru’ne, sigils, cards and basin exclusivity. MK173 story choices never silently alter foundational laws. Track ACCEPTED, WORKING and OPEN distinctly.
- Vera audits continuity/provenance; Astraeus develops story; Codex implements repository changes. Do not silently overwrite history.

## WORKING CRAFT TARGETS
Primarily Aren viewpoint, occasional Conservator/supporting viewpoints; 85/10/5 split illustrative only. Book 110k–150k words, arc 22k–30k, chapter generally 2.5k–4k, all flexible. Seven titles and 35 arc headings remain WORKING in `narrative/*/ARCS.md`.

## PRODUCTION WORKFLOW
Arc concept and stakes → AERA/chronology/geography audit → chapter/scene plan → draft → Vera continuity/terminology/provenance audit → update decision ledger and World Clock. No prose chapters yet; no fabricated math proofs, realm ratios, dates, exchange rates or distances.
