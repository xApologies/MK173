# MK173 — Development 09: Environmental Geography
Source: Genesis/Astraeus workshop, 2026-10-24. Separate ACCEPTED structure from WORKING geographical signatures.

## ACCEPTED — WHITE
White is the material superposition, not a seventh chromatic energy basin. Aren's planet has ~**80,000-mile circumference**, not diameter; major mountain elevations ~**20,000–30,000 FEET**, not miles. Exceptional Genesis-supported trees may reach miles high. Ordinary local spatial orientation, vast rivers/lakes/forests, protected city clusters (3–5 cities) and Wilds. City stabilization sigils suppress local Genesis collapse; they do not block existing Eldris as physical walls. Genesis storms, Domai, Eldris, dungeons and Genesis Mines exist. Dungeons are environmental prisms, not inherently collapse events. Planetary gravity/density and map projection OPEN.

## ACCEPTED — CHROMATIC REALMS
Six inhabited realms (Red through Violet) have ~80% dominant chromatic basin and 20% remaining scaffold for MK173. All six have **locally navigable yet globally non-ordinary geometry**; no globally mandatory up/down or single ground/sky. Opposing peaks, joined surfaces, orientation-changing paths and unexpected adjacency are possible in every realm. These features are not exclusive to a particular color; complete chromatic configuration matters. Natural navigable Corridors/Portals can bridge distinct White material planets. Chromatic natives visit home realm and White only; humans can visit all six. Humanoid form in White does not change identity/access.

## WORKING — CHARACTERISTIC REALM GEOGRAPHY
- Red/Dragons — Connected Heights: mountain systems, suspended or inverted connected surfaces, enormous citadels and relational bridges.
- Orange/Vaelith — Living Continuum: branching manifolds, living networks, coherent independent ecosystems; not one universal conscious organism.
- Yellow/Primordials — Transforming Lands: evolving geology and morphological continuity, stable habitable regions.
- Green/Sprites — Recursive Wilds: nested ecological scales, mile-high flora, intricate habitats, also plains and lakes.
- Blue/Leviathans — Enduring Expanse: colossal persistent environments, oceans and locally differently oriented water/land; not exclusively aquatic biology.
- Violet/Unicorns — Folded Horizons: nontrivial global topology, orientation-reversing routes, adjacency cartography; not exclusive ownership of nonordinary geometry.
