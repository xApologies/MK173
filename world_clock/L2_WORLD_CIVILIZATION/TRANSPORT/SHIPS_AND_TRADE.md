# Transport, trade and maritime design

**ACCEPTED:** Principal sea vessels are wooden and sail-free, purpose-built with hull-integrated sigil arrays. Activated arrays move water around the hull, producing propulsive force. Their operation remains subject to AERA Transduction, available energy, and structural constraints. Exact dimensions, knots, flow equations and ship classes remain OPEN; earlier proposed numeric ranges are not canon.

Timber is premium relative to mass-grown stone; protected noble forests supply timber, shipwrights fabricate wooden hulls, and specialists integrate sigils. Large ships are expensive capital assets. Large metal-hulled fleets are not part of the ordinary pre-industrial economy even though Type 1 metal growth exists.

Merchant convoys, escorts, armadas, shipyards, ports, customs, tariffs, marine megafauna and visiting intelligent Blue-realm Leviathans may shape ocean voyages. A visiting Leviathan is a person, not automatically a hostile monster. Avoidance, route choice and escorting can be preferable to enormous hull size.

Land travel uses roads and, where available, established spatial Corridor anchors and caravan staging; do not collapse AERA Corridor/Portal/Rainbow Road into unrestricted teleportation. Ocean crossings are enormous on the ~80,000-mile-circumference world. Actual routes and travel times OPEN.
