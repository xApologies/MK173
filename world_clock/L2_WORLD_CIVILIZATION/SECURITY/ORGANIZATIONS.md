# Criminal organizations — source-bounded MK173 adaptation

**Bandits:** localized road and Wilds predation, mobile camps, caravan threats and escort economy.
**Cartels:** regional black markets and deniable financial leverage, debt, trade dependencies, monopolies and influence. The source's fulcrum destabilization doctrine describes indirect exploitation of vulnerabilities, not open territorial sovereignty.
**Pirates:** organized maritime crime, raiding, illicit shipping, possible fleets and international black-market connections. Cartel/pirate relationships can be transactional; not necessarily one hierarchy.
**No required evil cabal of maegi.**
