# Land, timber, food, stone and alchemy

**ACCEPTED direction:** Dense stone cities rely on protected countryside (illustratively 100–300 mile stabilization reach, not a universal surveyed radius). Noble estates lease or operate fields, vineyards, herds and forestry. City stabilization infrastructure is independent of the ancient weaving network and does not constitute a physical wall against existing Eldris.

Outside protected domains, livestock and logging face Eldris, storms, unusual fauna and transport risks. Meat is expensive, not prohibited; diets predominantly plant-based. Noble managed forestry makes timber moderately available, while costlier than mass-produced stone. Fine wood interiors and timber mansions signal wealth; timber ships are expensive.

Traditional sigil arrays can grow ordinary Type 1 materials. Precisely grown stone blocks, ru’ne inscriptions and spatial locking allow modular and monumental coherent masonry. Blocks need not literally fuse into one atomic crystal to behave as a coherent engineered assembly. Material growth does not itself provide industrial cranes, foundries, large-beam handling, mass steel hull fabrication or repair facilities. Type 2–5 alchemy remains specialist Genesis material science; vaen is identity-bearing and not simply an element.

Agriculture inherits 17 slices x 13 crop positions from MK163/AERA, not an invented 221-name list. Alchemical soils and growth-field velis can improve production without removing nutrients, labor and resource costs. Abecca (A-B-E-C-C-A) coffeehouse culture and alchemical beverages/medicine are available; exact formulations and character habits OPEN.
